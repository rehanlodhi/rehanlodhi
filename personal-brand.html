Bio

Background

I'm a full-stack developer and systems engineer with seven years of professional experience building software across the stack — from database schemas to CLI tools to browser extensions. I grew up in Karachi and studied computer science at FAST-NUCES before moving into industry.

My work has ranged from early-stage product engineering at seed-stage startups to infrastructure work at companies with millions of users. I care deeply about the craft of software: the kind where the abstractions are honest, the error messages are human, and the thing actually works in production.

Outside of client work, I maintain several open-source projects and write regularly about systems thinking, developer tooling, and the intersection of design and engineering.

What I Do

I specialise in developer tooling, API design, and backend systems. I'm particularly interested in problems where performance, correctness, and ergonomics all matter at once — the kind of hard tradeoffs that most frameworks paper over.

On the frontend side, I work primarily with React and Next.js, with a strong bias toward accessibility and minimal runtime overhead. I've built design systems from scratch, shipped production Figma-to-code pipelines, and led the frontend architecture for a B2B SaaS used by 40+ enterprise clients.
