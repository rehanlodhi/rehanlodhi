Against premature Kubernetes. A pragmatic argument for RDS, EC2, and S3 when you have six users.

Every engineering team I've talked to in the last two years has gone through the same arc: start on a managed platform, get embarrassed by it during a funding conversation, migrate to Kubernetes, spend four months debugging cluster networking, wonder why they did this. The boring path is faster.
