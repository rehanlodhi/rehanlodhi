What cockpit design research taught me about information hierarchy in dashboards and developer tools.

Cockpit designers have spent decades solving problems that most software designers are still discovering. The core insight: attention is finite, error recovery is expensive, and the interface is not the product — the outcome is. Applied to dashboards and dev tools, this reframes almost every default decision.
